# Postbell design system

The approved direction uses Quartr's restrained institutional design language while keeping Postbell's identity, content, and product interface original.

## Principles

- Use a warm cream canvas for the landing page and an after-hours dark workspace for the product.
- Let scale, spacing, photography, and product evidence create interest.
- Keep navigation compact and writing direct.
- Present live signals on a continuous market canvas with chart, research trace, and evidence visibly connected.
- Keep product navigation in a compact top rail across Morning Brief, Night Tape, and Saved Research.
- Use original architectural or after-hours editorial photography.
- Link conclusions visibly to their sources.
- Avoid gradients, neon crypto styling, glass effects, decorative numbering, and generic card mosaics.
