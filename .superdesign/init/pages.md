## `/postbell.html` — Postbell visual prototype

Entry: `src/public/postbell.html`

Dependencies:

- `src/public/postbell.css`
- `src/public/postbell.js`

The Postbell page renders a top session bar, left overnight watchlist, central market chart and evidence cards, and a right analyst/decision rail. At narrow widths these regions stack vertically.
