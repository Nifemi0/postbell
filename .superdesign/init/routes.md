# Routes

The Express server serves `src/public` as static files.

| URL | Entry | Styles | Behavior |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/` | `src/public/index.html` | `src/public/style.css` | `src/public/app.js` |
| `/postbell.html` | `src/public/postbell.html` | `src/public/postbell.css` | `src/public/postbell.js` |

`/` is the original NexusTrader crypto terminal. `/postbell.html` is a standalone Bitget hackathon concept for an AI trading desk focused on tokenized US equities.
